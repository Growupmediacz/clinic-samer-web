New in our practice

8K ultrasound: your baby’s face before birth

Alongside 3D and 4D ultrasound we now offer 8K imaging. A scan taken during the examination becomes a realistic picture of your baby’s face – a keepsake to take home.

Book an ultrasound +420 734 421 860

8K imaging is an illustrative visualisation created by computer processing of the ultrasound scan. It is not a diagnostic image and your baby’s actual appearance may differ. The result depends on the baby’s position and the week of pregnancy.

Good to know before your first visit

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. You can book online or by phone. When booking, briefly tell us what brings you in so that we can choose the right type of visit.

Do you speak English?

Yes. The doctor speaks Czech, English, French, Italian and Arabic.

I am not covered by Czech public health insurance. Can I come?

Yes. We work with PVZP and UNIQA insurance for foreigners; otherwise you can pay for care directly. We always tell you the price in advance.

Can my partner come with me?

Yes. Your partner is welcome at consultations and selected examinations, if you wish and the nature of the examination allows it.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is usually needed for a standard gynaecological examination.
