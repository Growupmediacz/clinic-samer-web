Care & services

From prevention to parenthood

Whether you are planning a baby, need help conceiving, are expecting, or are looking for long-term gynaecological care – together we will find the right next step.

Book a visit
01

Pregnancy care & ultrasound

Usually every 3 weeks · partner welcome · covered by insurance

Continuous care with one doctor for the whole pregnancy. Ultrasound check-ups usually take place every three weeks, and genetic screening is recommended according to the stage of pregnancy. Your partner is welcome to join you. Regular check-ups are covered by Czech public health insurance; the prenatal package with genetic tests and 4D ultrasound costs CZK 2,500. New: 8K imaging – a realistic keepsake picture of your baby’s face created from the ultrasound scan (illustrative, not a diagnostic image).

02

Fertility care

Initial consultation ~45 min · for women and couples

We start with a thorough conversation and diagnostics for both the woman and the man. We explain the results in plain language and choose the next step together – from treatment in our practice to referral to a specialised assisted-reproduction centre. The initial consultation takes about 45 minutes and costs from CZK 2,500.

Book this service Details in Czech
03

General gynaecology

Preventive check-up ~30 min · covered by insurance

Preventive examinations including cervical screening, regular check-ups and care for acute and long-term gynaecological problems, contraception counselling, HPV vaccination, breast examination and STI testing. A preventive check-up takes about 30 minutes and is covered by Czech public health insurance.

Book this service Details in Czech
04

Intrauterine devices (IUD)

Consultation, insertion and follow-up check

We insert hormonal intrauterine systems (including Levosert) as well as non-hormonal devices. The doctor will help you choose the right option based on your health, age and family plans. The price depends on the type of device and is always communicated in advance.

Book this service Details in Czech
05

Hormonal disorders

Individual consultation based on symptoms and results

Diagnosis and treatment of hormonal problems in girls and women from about 15 to 50 years of age – irregular periods, PCOS, raised prolactin, thyroid function, ovarian reserve (AMH) and menopausal symptoms. Blood samples are taken directly in the practice.

Book this service Details in Czech
06

Weight & metabolic clinic

Initial consultation and individual care plan

Specialist care for excess weight, obesity and metabolic problems that can affect hormonal balance, fertility and the course of pregnancy. After an initial consultation we prepare an individual care plan.

Book this service Details in Czech
07

CO₂ laser gynaecology

Package of 3 sessions · CZK 7,000

Gentle laser treatment of post-partum changes, chronic vaginal discomfort, age-related changes of the vaginal tissue and mild urinary incontinence. Suitability is always assessed individually by the doctor first. A package of three sessions costs CZK 7,000 and is not covered by insurance.

Book this service Details in Czech
08

Online consultation

Video call · CZK 1,500

A video consultation with the doctor – suitable for discussing results, next steps or a second opinion when no physical examination is needed. The consultation costs CZK 1,500 and is booked and paid via ZnámýLékař.

Book this service Details in Czech

Prices may vary depending on the complexity of the procedure. We always inform you about the price of self-paid examinations in advance.
