A doctor who knows your story

MUDr. Samer Asad

Board-certified gynaecologist and specialist in reproductive medicine

Every path to parenthood is different. That is why the doctor builds care on a personal relationship, thorough explanation and continuity between examinations.

Book a visit

Care that builds on itself

From the first visit the doctor knows your story, follows how things develop and helps you make decisions with greater confidence. He sees neither pregnancy nor fertility treatment as a mere series of examinations, but as an important stage in the life of a woman and her partner.

He has been practising for more than 20 years and speaks Czech, English, French, Italian and Arabic.

Qualifications

  • Specialist qualification in gynaecology and obstetrics
  • Specialisation in assisted reproduction
  • Licence for independent practice
  • ZnámýLékař “Best Quality of Care” award 2022–2025
Your first contact and support

Nataliia Asad, DiS.

The nurse is often the first person you meet. She helps with scheduling, preparing for examinations and practical questions about your visit. During examinations she provides sensitive assistance and makes sure you feel calm and safe.
